## Kanjiskole screenshots
`src/photos/ks-*.webp` are screenshots of [Kanjiskole](https://github.com/Lena-LSG/Kanjiskole).
They show data from the KANJIDIC2 and JMdict dictionaries by the
[Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), used under
CC BY-SA 4.0, and are licensed as in the Kanjiskole repository: GPL-3.0-only AND CC-BY-SA-4.0.
